Official Grand Prix Rules & Regulations

1

6-Lap Superspeedway Sprint

The championship runs for exactly 6 full-throttle laps around the high-banked oval. Cars battle wheel-to-wheel in real-time circuit telemetry from green flag to checkered finish.

2

Pole Grid Qualification

Starting grid positions are locked based on your Monthly Close % and Demo Conversion. Setting the pace across both metrics secures the inside Pole Position front row.

3

Slipstream & DRS Slingshots

Tucking directly behind a rival car within draft distance reduces drag and ignites spark trails, triggering a high-velocity slingshot burst to complete an aggressive overtake.

4

Upgrading Your Race Car

Reinvest your earned engineering budget into high-performance parts. Equip hybrid MGU-K units, carbon front wings, titanium DRS flaps, and Pirelli slicks in the Driver Garage.

5

KPI Performance & Hazards

Floor KPIs actively dictate car capabilities and on-track risks. Strong numbers fund faster builds, while lapses in critical metrics trigger mechanical derates, spin-outs, or terminal DNFs.

6

The Foundation Grand Prix

Drivers battle in 4 Constructor Teams. Every top-10 finish scores official F1 World Championship points (25-18-15-12...) toward your squad's Constructors Title on the victory podium.

How to Upgrade Your Race Car • Quick Step-by-Step

1

Close Deals & Hit Milestones

Execute demos, close sales, and collect 5-star customer reviews on the floor. Every achieved KPI milestone deposits cold engineering cash into circulation.

2

Dual Split & Banking

Your earned earnings split automatically: the majority funds your personal Driver Wallet, while a set share deposits directly into your squad's shared Factory Bank.

3

Unlock Shared Factory Perks

Pooled funds unlock team-wide upgrades: Wind Tunnel (+4% Top Speed), ERS Power (+8% Accel), and Reliability Shields (-50% Hazard Risk) for you and all 3 teammates.

4

Access Your Driver Garage

Navigate to the Paddock tab, locate your car on the driver roster, and click Enter Garage using your secure 4-digit personal driver PIN.

5

Equip Individual Parts

Spend personal wallet cash on performance components: Twin Turbos, Carbon Front Wings, DRS Drag-Reduction Flaps, Carbon Brakes, or Pirelli SuperSoft Slicks.

6

Calibrate & Hit the Track

Your individual parts combine with active Factory perks to boost your top speed, launch throttle, and corner grip for yourself and your Constructor team!
